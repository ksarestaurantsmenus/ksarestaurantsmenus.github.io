KSA Restaurants Menus With Prices (2026 Updated Guide )

Introduction

Saudi Arabia offers a rich and diverse food scene. You can find global chains and traditional kitchens in every city. Menus and prices change often due to demand and offers. This guide shares updated restaurant menus across the Kingdom. You will see prices, meal types, and smart comparisons. The goal is simple: Help you choose the right meal fast.

Popular Fast Food Restaurants Menus in KSA

AlBaik Menu With Prices

AlBaik remains a top choice for affordable fried chicken. The menu focuses on chicken meals, nuggets, and shrimp. Most meals include fries, bread, and garlic sauce. Prices stay lower than many global chains. Family boxes offer better value for groups.

McDonald’s Saudi Arabia Menu

McDonald’s offers burgers, fries, and combo meals. Popular items include Big Mac, McChicken, and McArabia. Happy Meals target kids with smaller portions. Combo meals include fries and a drink. Seasonal deals appear often and reduce total cost.

KFC Saudi Arabia Menu

KFC focuses on fried chicken buckets and meals. Zinger burgers and Twister wraps attract younger customers. Family buckets include multiple chicken pieces and sides. Spicy and original flavors give variety. Large meals suit sharing and group dining.

Hardee’s & Burger King Menu

These brands focus on flame-grilled burgers. Premium burgers include Angus and double beef options. Combo meals include fries and drinks. Value meals cost less and suit quick eating. Desserts and shakes add extra appeal.

Local Saudi Restaurants Menu List

Shawarma & Arabic Food Menus

Shawarma remains a daily favorite across cities. Menus include chicken shawarma, beef wraps, and platters. Garlic sauce and pickles enhance the taste. Prices stay affordable for most people. Combo deals include fries and drinks.

Traditional Saudi Food Menus

Traditional dishes reflect culture and heritage. Kabsa and mandi remain popular rice meals. Madghout offers rich flavor with spices. Meals often serve large portions for sharing. Family platters provide better value than single meals.

Restaurant Menu Prices in Major Cities

Riyadh Restaurant Menus

Riyadh offers a wide mix of premium and budget options. Prices stay slightly higher in busy areas. Fast food chains keep consistent pricing across branches.

Jeddah Restaurant Menus

Jeddah shows strong demand for seafood and fast food. Prices vary based on location and tourism traffic. Deals and offers appear more often near busy spots.

Dammam & Khobar Menus

These cities offer balanced pricing and variety. Family restaurants remain very popular here. Menus often include grilled and rice-based meals.

Dine-In vs Takeaway Menu Prices in KSA

Dine-in prices may include extra service costs. Takeaway orders often cost slightly less. Some deals apply only to app orders. VAT usually appears in the final bill. Always check before placing a large order.

Healthy & Calorie-Friendly Menu Options

Many restaurants now offer lighter meal choices. Grilled chicken costs less in calories than fried food. Salads and wraps provide balanced options. Avoid large fries and sugary drinks. Choose water or juice for better health.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ )

What is the average cost of food in KSA restaurants?

A basic meal costs a low to medium price. Premium meals cost more in busy areas.

Which is the cheapest restaurant in Saudi Arabia?

Local shawarma shops often offer the lowest prices. Some chicken chains also provide budget meals.

Are restaurant prices the same in all cities?

Prices vary slightly between major cities. Busy locations may charge more than smaller areas.

Where can I check updated menus in KSA?

Use delivery apps and official restaurant apps. Maps and reviews also show updated menu photos.

Do restaurants in Saudi Arabia include VAT in menu prices?

Some menus include VAT in the listed price. Others add VAT at checkout. Always check before paying.
